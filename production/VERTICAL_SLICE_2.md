# Vertical Slice 2.0 — Tethys / Kestra

## Target uninterrupted experience
1. Step out of the ship onto Tethys.
2. Read the environment without a wall of HUD.
3. Verify atmosphere through an interaction-driven tutorial.
4. Encounter and investigate wildlife/flora.
5. Locate and physically gather one useful fuel resource.
6. Notice evidence of Talari civilization before a formal introduction.
7. Reach one dense Kestra district.
8. Interact with inhabitants performing ordinary routines.
9. Investigate one archaeological site with evidence from multiple eras.
10. Discover a contradiction in accepted local history.
11. Return to the ship.
12. Launch manually and fly over the settlement.
13. Land again with strong contact, audio, dust, and landing feedback.

## Success condition
Ten uninterrupted minutes of this slice should look, sound, control, and feel credible beside a high-quality 2026 commercial game at the project's chosen scope, without relying on feature count to create the impression of quality.
