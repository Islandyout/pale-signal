# Free Toolchain Policy

Preferred candidates. Specialists must verify current license/terms before adoption.

- Godot — production engine candidate
- Blender — modeling, rigging, animation
- Krita — concept art, paintover, UI art
- Material Maker — procedural/PBR materials
- Audacity — sound editing
- Poly Haven — CC0 assets where suitable
- Kenney — free/CC0 prototyping assets where suitable
- GitHub Free — repository/issues/PR coordination within free allowances

## Rules
- No paid marketplace assets.
- No paid plugins.
- No subscription middleware.
- No tool whose useful export is locked behind payment.
- No free trial as a production dependency.
- All downloaded assets require a license ledger entry before use.
